Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Plex On handles information when you use our media and video player application.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Plex On (“Plex On”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). Plex On is a media and video player application designed to let users play supported media stored on their devices and access supported network or streaming media.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only information that is necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.

Important: Plex On does not require you to create an account unless a particular version or feature explicitly says otherwise.

2. Information We May Access

2.1 Media and files on your device

Depending on your Android version and the permissions you grant, Plex On may access media files that you choose to play, such as video, audio, or other supported media files.

Access to media is used to provide playback and related player functionality. Plex On does not need to upload your personal media files to our servers simply for local playback.

2.2 Network and streaming content

Plex On may connect to the internet or a local network when you choose to play content from a network location, streaming URL, media server, or another supported source.

The content and network address you choose to access may be processed by the app as necessary to establish and maintain playback.

2.3 Technical information

Like most applications, certain technical information may be automatically available to the app or its service providers, such as device type, operating-system version, app version, network connectivity information, and diagnostic information.

Such information may be used where necessary for security, troubleshooting, compatibility, performance, and service operation.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Plex On is designed primarily as a media player. We do not intentionally request or collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, contacts, SMS messages, or precise location solely for ordinary media playback.

If a future feature requires additional personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that feature is introduced, where required by applicable law.

4. How Information Is Used

Information available to Plex On may be used to:

  • Provide video, audio, and media playback.
  • Connect to supported network or streaming sources.
  • Maintain app functionality and compatibility.
  • Diagnose crashes, bugs, and technical problems.
  • Protect the app and its users against abuse, fraud, or security threats.
  • Improve performance and reliability where permitted.
  • Send notifications if you have enabled or permitted notification functionality.

5. Firebase and Push Notifications

Plex On may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), to deliver push notifications.

If FCM is enabled in the version of Plex On you use, Google may process technical information and a device/app identifier or registration token required to deliver notifications.

Notification permission can be controlled through Android settings where supported.

6. Advertising

Plex On is intended to support advertising through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. Advertising is currently disabled in the present version of the app. If advertising is enabled in a future release, the applicable advertising SDKs and data practices will apply as described in this section.

Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network information, and ad interaction information according to their own policies and applicable settings.

Where required, Plex On will request applicable permissions or consent and provide available choices regarding personalized advertising.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Plex On may use analytics or crash-reporting services to understand app stability and diagnose technical problems.

These services may process technical information such as app version, device information, operating-system information, crash logs, and diagnostic events.

Developer note: Keep this section only if your production build actually uses an analytics or crash-reporting SDK, such as Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

8. Third-Party Services

Plex On may rely on third-party services to provide certain technical functions. These services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google Play Services — Android platform and app services.
  • Firebase — only for Firebase services actually enabled in Plex On.
  • Google AdMob — only if advertising is enabled.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

9. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate app functionality, provide notifications, display advertisements, diagnose technical problems, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

The exact information processed depends on the services enabled in the version of Plex On you are using.

10. Data Storage and Retention

Media files that you play locally remain on your device unless you choose to move, copy, upload, or otherwise share them using another application or service.

Technical or diagnostic information handled by third-party services may be retained for the period necessary for their stated purposes, security, legal requirements, or legitimate business needs.

Plex On does not retain your local media files on our own servers merely because you play them through the app.

11. Data Deletion and Your Choices

You can control Android permissions through your device settings. You can also stop using Plex On or uninstall the application at any time.

If Plex On stores account or server-side personal information in a future feature, you may request deletion of that information by contacting us using the email address below, subject to legal and security requirements.

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data according to Android's app-data behavior, but it does not necessarily delete data already retained by independent third-party service providers.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by Plex On against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic transmission, storage system, or internet connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

Plex On is not specifically directed to children under the age applicable under local law, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

14. External Links and Content

Plex On may allow you to access media, streaming URLs, network resources, or services operated by third parties. Those third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites, servers, media sources, or applications that you choose to access.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Plex On's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be changed accordingly.
